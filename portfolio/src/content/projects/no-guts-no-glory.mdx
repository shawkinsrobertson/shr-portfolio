### What It Is

No Guts No Glory is a personalized gastritis symptom and risk trend tracking mobile application made for a loved one who eschews wearables and wanted a quick way to assess flare-up risk based on known stressors, recovery factors, and symptoms. It is customizable and intended for general trends only, not medical guidance.

### What It Solves

Provides a user-friendly daily tracker and trends for gastritis stressors and recovery factors.

### How It Does It

Users weight stress and recovery factors on a 1–10 scale. The recovery total is capped at a 30% reduction in overall stress load per day. The calculated load is rolled into a 72-hour average and tracked against a user-set target (approximately 50% of total possible load). The rolling average is plotted across weeks on a Trends tab, and data can be exported for a clinician as observed stressors and trends to inform management.

### Features

- List of common stressors, recovery factors, and symptoms, with options to add custom entries and adjust the overall stress/load target
- Fully customizable weights for stressors and recovery factors
- Quick-tap interface for daily logging
- Rolling 72-hour load average with long-term trends tab
- PDF export report of rolling load averages and most common stressors

### What I Did

**Customizable inputs:** Gastritis stress and recovery factors are highly individual and can change over time. Weights and values (stressors, recovery factors, and symptoms) can be edited after onboarding, and users can adjust their stress target as their experience and relationship to their condition changes.

**Quick, efficient tracking flow:** Designed tracking to be a light lift amid the many demands of health tracking. Daily notifications surface common stressors for logging in a few taps without opening the app. In-app logging is similarly quick: tapping a stressor once registers “mild,” and one or two additional taps change it to “moderate” or “severe” (or back to “mild”). Missed logs appear at the top of the logbook as buttons for easy catch-up.
